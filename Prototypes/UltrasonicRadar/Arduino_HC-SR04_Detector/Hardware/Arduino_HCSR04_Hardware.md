# Ultrasonic Single Sensor Prototype

## Objective
The objective of this practice was to understand how a sensor with a transmitter "TX" and a receiver "RX" piezoelectric transducers. One emits the sound, and the other receive it. It works by measuring the time elapsed between the emission of the ultrasonic pulse and its echo, when an object in its trajectory into the receptor. This time is multiplied my the speed of sound and divided into two. 
This principle is similarly applied in the 5.8GHz radar, but it uses electromagnetic waves. Thus it involves the speed of light, beat frequency and bandwidth. Please go to [README](/README.md) to learn the specific formulas and concepts.
## Hardware Setup

| Pin       | Component        | Function       | Purpose |
| :-------: | :--------------: | :------------: | :------ |
| Pin 10    | HC-SR04 Sensor   | Trigger        | Sends the ultrasonic pulse |
| Pin 9     | HC-SR04 Sensor   | Echo           | Receives the reflected pulse |
